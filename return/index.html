Return is the place for scraps that are not ready to be a project. Grok ties them when you ask. You keep, edit, or throw the proposal out.

  1. 01

    Dump

    One scrap. Enter. No project field, no date field. The point is to get it out of your head.

  2. 02

    Loose

    What is still untied. Pin it, tie it by hand, or hand the pile to Grok.

  3. 03

    Compile

    Grok groups related scraps, names a project, and sets a date only when the words imply one. Personal notes stay out of the work threads.

  4. 04

    Brief, then decide

    One pass over the desk: in motion, blocked, dates, leave alone. Then a single move before you leave.
